PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: May 31, 2026. How we manage and secure your personal guest profiles.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Luxe Aura Salon & Spa respects your right to data privacy. We collect personal information you explicitly provide when reserving self-care treatments, subscribing to our magazine newsletter, or contacting us through online forms.

This data includes your full name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment times, and specific stylist notes or skin allergy profiles.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Your details are strictly utilized to coordinate and execute personalized self-care sessions, process transaction bookings, and occasionally transmit private invitations or event updates.

We do not sell, rent, or lease guest records to third-party marketing entities. Access to your profile is limited strictly to certified Luxe Aura staff.

3. SECURITY & RETENTION

We apply advanced industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS) to safeguard all data entered on our reservation forms. Payment details are fully managed through PCI-DSS certified payment gateways.

We retain your records only for as long as necessary to fulfill your booked appointments or legally required tax records.

4. YOUR DATA RIGHTS

You retain full rights to request access to your guest profile, correct inaccuracies, or request complete removal of your records from our systems at any time. Simply transmit your request to hello@luxeaurasalon.com.

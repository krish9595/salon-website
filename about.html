The Vision

A SANCTUARY BORN OUT OF OBSESSION

Luxe Aura was established in 2014 by award-winning stylist Elena Vance and holistic health advocate Sophia Rossi. The goal was singular: to disrupt the local salon industry by introducing standard-setting salon excellence merged with immersive luxury brand aesthetics.

We recognized that high-net-worth clients and modern professionals did not just seek quick haircut or color updates—they sought a rejuvenating transition. A place where time slows down, details are obsessed over, and every guest feels like an exclusive muse.

From selecting organic, toxic-free shampoos to custom engineering individual massage scent oils, every square inch of our Madison Avenue sanctuary is designed with pure intent. We call this the "Luxe Aura Experience".

Founder's Note

BEAUTY IS AN INTERNAL STATE

Our Journey

Trace the milestones that established Luxe Aura as a multi-million-dollar beauty and lifestyle brand.

2018
